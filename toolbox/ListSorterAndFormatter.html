What is the List Sorter?

Whether you need to alphabetize a names list, remove duplicate items, change custom delimiters, or reverse an array, organizing messy lists manually is tedious. This utility parses your items using custom text boundaries, applies sorting rules, strips duplicates instantly, and reformats delimiters on the fly.

Simple tool designed for my own use, but free for anyone to use.

Features:

  • Custom Text Delimiters: Split and join items using any character, string, or literal like `\n`.
  • Smart Deduplication: Removes duplicate entries automatically with optional case-insensitivity.
  • Multiple Sort Orders: Sort lists alphabetically, by text length, or reverse the original entry order instantly.
Processed locally in your browser. No data leaves your machine.
